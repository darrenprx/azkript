EXT. ROOFTOP - DUSK

Wind pulls at a line of antennae. A radio lies on the gravel, its dial glowing.

MARA

(into the radio)

This is Mara. If anyone can hear me, say something now.

Static. Then, under it, a voice:

MARA

Who is this?

VOICE

Someone who has been waiting.

CUT TO:

INT. RADIO STATION - NIGHT

Rows of dead monitors. One screen wakes and paints the room blue.
